## Items that are *not* defects (verified)
- `/admin/dashboard` aliases the command centre by design; `/admin/support` aliases `/admin/support-tickets`.
- Source-level route guard, sidebar permission filter, request-sequencing in `AdminView.load` and the independent-source `Promise.allSettled` pattern are correct and must be preserved.
- The Phase 12 token aliases resolve; the visual failure is the missing canvas/vocabulary, not an undefined variable.
