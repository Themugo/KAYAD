# 01 — Dealer workspace audit
Entry: `/?nav=dealer-dashboard[&section=…]` → `src/pages/dealer/dashboard/DealerDashboard.tsx` (lazy in `App.tsx`; `/dealer` redirects to it). Non-dealer, non-admin accounts are redirected away (App.tsx ~225): team members (personal accounts) use `?nav=dealership-workspace` (`features/DealershipWorkspace`).
Sections (single sidebar `DEALER_NAV_GROUPS`, drawer on mobile): overview, inventory, leads, pipeline, customers, auctions, inspections, escrow, finance, analytics, reputation, marketing, team, banking, settings. Other routes: `/dealer/onboarding`, `/dealer/auction-setup`, `/dealer/auction-operations[/:id]`, `/dealer/setup?invite=` (team invitation landing).
