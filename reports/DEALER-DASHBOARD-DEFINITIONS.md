# Dealer dashboard — metric definitions
Period = last 7, 30 or 90 days (default 30); "previous period" = the equal window immediately before it.
- **Total Inventory**: all of the dealership's listings. Badge: listings created in the window.
- **Active Leads**: leads in New, Contacted, Negotiating, Inspection booked or Reserved. Badge: leads received vs the previous window.
- **Sales**: escrows released to the dealer in the window. **Payouts**: payouts paid in the window (owner only).
- **Trend badge**: only when a previous-period figure exists; a percentage only when previous > 0; otherwise absolute or "New".
- **Inventory quality**: share of published listings with a price and ≥ 1 photo. **Listing performance**: share of published listings viewed ≥ 1 time.
- **Lead response (≤ 2 hrs)**: NOT AVAILABLE — response times are not recorded; shown as "—".
- **Account compliance**: 100 % when dealer verification is approved, "In review" when pending, "—" otherwise.
- "—" = unavailable; a genuine zero is 0; a skeleton = loading.
